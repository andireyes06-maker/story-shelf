## Kasane's Arc 1 Want / Need / Movement

### 18.15 Arc 1 Want / Need / Character Movement

- **Internal want:** to understand what happened well enough that it stops disrupting her life — deliberately modest; she wants the strange thing to acquire rules, because a thing with rules can be worked around.
- **External want, in order:** survive the Marking → reach civilization → avoid being treated as a Gate-creature → learn what happened to her eyes → complete registration → understand the Stitch → stop powerful people from deciding her future without her.
- **Need:** to learn that some problems can't be contained from outside them — her instinct is always to manage the fire at a remove; Arc 1 forces the recognition that she's already inside the kiln.
- **Movement, beat by beat:** *Beginning* — "something happened to me, I need to understand it and get my life back." *Early middle* — "the people who claim to understand this don't understand it either." *Late middle* — "some of them know that and are hiding it." *Climax* — forced into a moment where using the Stitch openly forecloses anonymity; she uses it anyway, not from accepting destiny but because not using it produces an outcome she can't accept. *End* — not heroic, **committed**: she's lost the possibility of returning to who she was, and gained the start of her own understanding of the world in its place.

## Yamashiro's Arc 1 Thematic Question and Political Pressure Map

### 12.9 Arc 1's Political Question, and the Per-Clan Pressure Map Around Kasane

🟢 Kasane doesn't enter Yamashiro politics wanting reform — she enters because the system can't decide what to do with her, which forces every institution to answer, in its own way, *does the system exist to interpret reality, or does reality have to conform to the system?* Concrete per-Clan pressure, usable as a quick reference for any political scene: Shirogane wants to **contain and classify** her; Kuromori wants to **determine her practical military value** (and would become one of her fiercest defenders if the Stitch can be shown to close a Rent, for entirely unsentimental reasons); Akaishi wants to **claim and reinterpret** her; Tsukishiro wants to **understand and leverage** her; Karasu wants to know **whether she can explain the Silence**; Motoi wants to determine **whether the anomaly is theological** before the Clans decide unilaterally. These interests overlap without requiring any faction to be cartoonishly evil — several genuinely competing, locally rational agendas converging on one person is enough conflict on its own.

## Arc 1 Macro-Spine — Seven Movements

### 19.2 Arc 1 Macro-Spine — Seven Movements

🟢 Not a scene-by-scene outline — the causal skeleton chapter sequencing should subdivide. Movements, not chapter counts; a movement may be one chapter or several, decided during actual chapter-sequencing, not here.

- **Movement I — The Wrong Eyes.** The Marking, the descent, the immediate aftermath. Chapter 1 lives here (Section 19.5 below).
- **Movement II — Classification.** The registration problem becomes visible; an institutional handler is assigned; first attempts to test/assess her; Clan awareness begins (Section 12.3's Boundary Rite pressure starts building here, off-page at first).
- **Movement III — Ownership.** Multiple factions begin actively interpreting or claiming her (Section 12.9's per-Clan pressure map becomes concrete, on-page pressure); Kasane tries to preserve some autonomy inside it. This is where her core intellectual flaw (Section 18.5) costs her for the first time, against Genzou (see `ARC_01_CAST.md`) — a reasonable read on him that's wrong not because she missed a clue, but because he chose something irrational.
- **Movement IV — The Rule That Doesn't Work.** Early Stitch experimentation collides with Gate/Ura contradictions (Section 8's Five Limits should start showing up concretely here) — mechanical, not relational; the flaw-cost lives in Movement III, not here.
- **Movement V — Pressure.** The Karasu situation, Boundary Rite mustering, and background Gate escalation (Section 11.13's economic/social-before-visual rule) begin intersecting with her personal problem rather than sitting in the background.
- **Movement VI — Public Choice.** Kasane is forced into a moment where using the Stitch openly forecloses anonymity — and uses it anyway, not from accepting destiny but because not using it produces an outcome she can't accept (this is Section 18.15's Arc 1 climax beat, now placed in the macro-spine).
- **Movement VII — Consequence.** She survives; anonymity doesn't. Yamashiro is changed, not fixed (Section 1.17's arc-ending discipline). Kasane ends Arc 1 committed, not heroic.

## Movement-by-Movement Detail

### Movement I — The Wrong Eyes

**Purpose:** The Marking, the descent, the immediate aftermath. Chapter 1 lives here — see `CHAPTER_OUTLINE.md`.

**Kasane's state entering:** Ordinary — a charcoal-burner managing a routine, weeks-long burn, alone.

**Primary pressure:** The Gate/Marking event itself; a burn left unattended and almost certainly ruined by her decision to descend.

**Institutional pressure:** None yet — pre-institutional. The town's inability to process what's in front of it is what launches Movement II.

**Stitch stage:** Pre-Stitch / onset. The Marking occurs; no deliberate Stitch use yet.

**Information revealed:** Kasane knows something Gate-like occurred, something happened to her specifically, and the System reacted wrong (see `CHAPTER_OUTLINE.md`'s knowledge gate for the full field).

**Information withheld:** Everything past Chapter 1's knowledge gate — the Erosion's role, her status as its unintended consequence, and all other author-only material listed there.

**Relationship movement:** The pre-Marking tie (Genzou) establishes what "ordinary Kasane" looked like to another person, per the knowledge-gating discipline — though he doesn't appear on-page in Chapter 1 itself; his thread activates in Movement II via ordinary market rumor.

**Required consequence:** The burn is lost — a concrete, ordinary economic cost, independent of anything institutional, which is what pulls Genzou into Movement II for his own reasons.

**State exiting:** A problem other people must interpret; anonymity is locally gone, not yet institutionally formalized.

### Movement II — Classification

**Purpose:** The registration problem becomes visible; an institutional handler is assigned; first attempts to test/assess her; Clan awareness begins building off-page.

**Kasane's state entering:** Shaken but functional and cooperative; wants competent people to explain what happened, because a thing with rules can be worked around.

**Primary pressure:** No income (the burn is gone); assessment interviews; uncertainty about what's being documented about her.

**Institutional pressure:** Tsukishiro (Aya) is assigned as first responder because information-handling is their specialty — not because jurisdiction is settled. Who ultimately has authority over her case remains genuinely open (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §12.3).

**Stitch stage:** First accidental, minor manifestation under stress — a **Slip** (`Bible/03_MAGIC_AND_SYSTEM.md` §8.4) — witnessed by Aya during an interview. An institutional Witnessed Use, not yet a public one.

**Information revealed:** To Aya and the case file: the anomaly is real and specific, well beyond "odd eyes." Kasane, cooperating fully, describes early Stitch sensations (§8.2) more completely than a warier person would.

**Information withheld:** Everything past Chapter 1's knowledge gate; Aya's own clan-loyalty tension (the reader may start to infer it, Kasane can't yet).

**Relationship movement:** The Institutional Handler (Aya) begins adversarial/assessing. Genzou re-enters independently here — market rumor about the Marking reaches him fast, and his forge-house contract gives him an urgent, self-interested reason to find out whether she can still supply him.

**Required consequence:** The case becomes too visible and specific to stay a quiet Tsukishiro-only matter; other Clans begin actively responding to it, not just hearing about it — feeding directly into Movement III.

**State exiting:** Formally "in the system" — a documented, witnessed anomaly — with no closer approach to having rules for her situation.

### Movement III — Ownership

**Purpose:** Multiple factions begin actively interpreting or claiming her; the per-Clan pressure map (below) becomes concrete, on-page pressure. Kasane tries to preserve some autonomy inside it. Her core intellectual flaw costs her here, against Genzou (see `ARC_01_CAST.md`).

**Kasane's state entering:** A documented anomaly with multiple parties now actively interested; still cooperative, increasingly aware that cooperation has costs.

**Primary pressure:** Renji's courtship (the hidden-blood theory made concrete); Isato assigned to determine her practical value; the Rite's approach making all of it time-pressured, without an absolute legal deadline.

**Institutional pressure:** Per the per-Clan pressure map (§12.9): Shirogane wants containment, Akaishi wants a claim, Kuromori wants a practical read, Karasu wants an answer to the Silence, Motoi wants a theological determination before the Clans act unilaterally — none of it requires any faction to be irrational.

**Stitch stage:** Still pre-deliberate-experimentation; this movement's failure is relational, not mechanical, by design — mechanical experimentation begins in earnest in Movement IV.

**Information revealed:** To the reader: Kasane's overconfidence in a correct-but-incomplete prediction about Genzou costs her something concrete, not because she lacked information but because she trusted a sound read too far.

**Information withheld:** Nothing protected is touched.

**Relationship movement:** The Veteran Hunter (Isato) and the Clan Heir/Rival (Renji) become live. Kasane's flaw costs her against Genzou — she preemptively cuts him out of a resource/logistics need to protect him from association with her case, committing to a costlier, less trustworthy alternative; he refuses to be protected and provides the help anyway, publicly, on his own account. Suzu's Consecration-prep question (does her shrine's procedure even apply to Kasane's case) also surfaces here, off to the side of the main pressure.

**Required consequence:** Shirogane's containment-minded faction uses the visible overlap between Kasane's alternative arrangement and Genzou's public intervention to justify tightening oversight of her movement and autonomy — closing off her caution-based strategy as a viable path forward.

**State exiting:** More restricted, and personally aware that protecting people she cares about can backfire institutionally — undermining her own want that this situation "acquire rules" she can manage from outside it.

### Movement IV — The Rule That Doesn't Work

**Purpose:** Early Stitch experimentation collides with Gate/Ura contradictions (the Five Limits, `Bible/03_MAGIC_AND_SYSTEM.md`, should start showing up concretely here) — mechanical, not relational; the flaw-cost lives in Movement III, not here.

**Kasane's state entering:** Restricted but cooperative; now actively motivated to understand and control the Stitch rather than just endure scrutiny, since her caution-based strategy lost ground in Movement III.

**Primary pressure:** Genuine physical risk and uncertainty in supervised trials conducted with Isato.

**Institutional pressure:** Isato pushes for decisive commitment during trials — sound doctrine in general, misapplied here (his established blind spot: conflating "needs more supervised practice" with "needs to stop hesitating").

**Stitch stage:** Early experimentation. The specific failure: Kasane wrongly assumes that superficially similar Ura-adjacent spaces behave consistently, and — under Isato's pressure to commit rather than double-check — reaches into a space she hasn't actually scouted. This violates the already-locked **Knowledge** limit (§8.3: "she can't safely Stitch into a place she understands nothing about"). The seam rejects the attempt: a **Rebound** (§8.4's existing failure taxonomy) — no new mechanic invented.

**Information revealed:** Isato's assessment file now holds real, firsthand evidence that she's genuinely bounded and dangerous-to-herself, not secretly withholding capability — data that shifts, without resolving, how the Clans read her going forward.

**Information withheld:** Nothing protected.

**Relationship movement:** Isato takes a first, partial step toward revising his method after witnessing the Rebound firsthand — consistent with his approved capacity to "genuinely shift," not resolve, his read of her caution.

**Required consequence:** She has now hit real limits in both available personal strategies — caution failed politically in Movement III, mastery hits a real wall here — with no third private option left to try.

**State exiting:** Technically limited, more closely watched, aware that neither caution nor mastery alone protects her.

### Movement V — Pressure

**Purpose:** The Karasu situation, Boundary Rite mustering, and background Gate escalation begin intersecting with her personal problem rather than sitting in the background.

**Kasane's state entering:** Technically limited, institutionally watched, aware that her own strategies are exhausted.

**Primary pressure:** Rising regional Gate activity as ordinary economic/social texture (§11.13) — charcoal and timber prices shifting, Kuromori's response teams stretched thinner, forge-house demand spiking early; the Rite's Muster/Registration functions closing in; Renji's own internal-Akaishi appointment competition peaking at the same event.

**Institutional pressure:** Kuromori (via Isato) wants a low-complication case, not a new one, while stretched thin; Shirogane wants registration resolved cleanly and is more impatient after Movement III's discrepancy; Karasu makes a genuine, sympathetic, bounded approach — not a demand that she visit the Silent Gate, but a request that she examine specific reported details or a transportable piece of evidence from their territory, within her real Contact/Knowledge limits.

**Stitch stage:** A bounded, Contact/Knowledge-limited examination of Karasu-related material — no travel to the Silent Gate, no resolution of what it means.

**Information revealed:** Pattern, not answer. Kasane's honest "I can't resolve this, and I might make it worse by guessing" is itself informative to Isato and Aya — evidence she isn't overclaiming capability. Karasu's mystery stays exactly where `Bible/04_CONTINUITY_AND_MYSTERIES.md` leaves it.

**Information withheld:** The Karasu Silence's actual explanation, and everything else protected.

**Relationship movement:** Isato spends real personal capital — standing in his rival-commander competition — to permit even this limited engagement. Suzu's Consecration-prep question becomes concrete and urgent as the Rite approaches. Renji's stakes intensify just as Kasane is least able to accommodate being seen with him.

**Required consequence:** Karasu's hope is disappointed, not resolved; Isato has spent real capital for an inconclusive outcome. Registration pressure, Renji's stakes, Shirogane's impatience, Karasu's disappointment, and Kuromori's stretched resources are all now simultaneously live, with no institutional slack left to absorb anything new.

**State exiting:** Maximally exposed, every faction's patience thinnest, right as the Rite itself begins.

### Movement VI — Public Choice

**Purpose:** Kasane is forced into a moment where using the Stitch openly forecloses anonymity — and uses it anyway, not from accepting destiny but because not using it produces an outcome she can't accept. This is the Arc 1 climax beat.

**Kasane's state entering:** At the Rite itself, watched, restricted, with every faction present at once.

**Primary pressure:** The unresolved Consecration question, seeded in Movements III and V via Suzu, comes to a head: a committee (a Clan-appointed cleric, Aya, and Suzu) attempts a modified consecration procedure for Kasane's registration, built on assumptions never tested against an anomaly like hers.

**Institutional pressure:** A genuinely well-intentioned, rational institutional attempt, not incompetence — it goes wrong because nobody could have known this specific site's Ura-significance in advance (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §12.1).

**Stitch stage:** A narrow, Contact-range, short-Duration stabilization — holding a localized instability steady long enough for evacuation. Not sealing (§8.10 — sealing requires knowledge she doesn't have), not a sweeping rescue.

**Information revealed:** Public and unambiguous: every Clan, the priesthood, and civilians present now witness what she is and what she can do. No protected lore is revealed — the reveal is social and institutional, not mechanical or ontological.

**Information withheld:** Everything about the Erosion, the late-saga ontology, and Karasu's true explanation remains untouched.

**Relationship movement:** Suzu is directly, publicly implicated in the failed original procedure. Isato and other Hunters attempt the standard evacuation/sealing response in parallel — reasonable, insufficient for this specific cause, not incompetent.

**Required consequence:** Anonymity ends completely and irreversibly, in front of everyone who matters institutionally, simultaneously.

**State exiting:** Having acted, at real physical cost, to prevent harm to people she has relationships with — no longer a private anomaly, now a witnessed fact every faction must respond to at once.

### Movement VII — Consequence

**Purpose:** She survives; anonymity doesn't. Yamashiro is changed, not fixed. Kasane ends Arc 1 committed, not heroic.

**Kasane's state entering:** Committed: she has lost the possibility of returning to who she was, and gained the start of her own understanding of the world in its place.

**Primary pressure:** Every Clan and the priesthood reacting simultaneously to the same witnessed fact, each from its own locally rational angle.

**Institutional pressure:** Shirogane pushes harder for containment; Kuromori, via a partially-vindicated Isato, becomes a genuine, unsentimental advocate for her practical value; Akaishi, via Renji, redoubles the hidden-blood claim with a dramatic public event to point to; Tsukishiro (Aya) finally has an evidence-grounded classification instead of a guess; Karasu renews hope she can help with the Silence — left open, not paid off; Motoi and the priesthood face a real theological reckoning without any doctrine being confirmed or debunked.

**Stitch stage:** Real but survivable cost from the Movement VI use — early/mid symptoms (§8.6/8.8), with perhaps the first faint, unconfirmed hint of the "less recoverable" cost category, seeding later saga material without resolving it.

**Information revealed:** Nothing protected — institutional and political facts only.

**Information withheld:** Karasu's mystery, the Erosion's nature, and the late-saga ontology all remain exactly as untouched as before.

**Relationship movement:** Genzou's Movement III rupture is dwarfed by bigger events but not erased. Isato's respect is earned and partial. Renji's stakes are now entangled with an event bigger than anything he engineered — left open whether that helps or hurts him. Suzu's Motoi-loyalty-vs-community tension becomes acute and unresolved. Aya has the case she wanted, at higher stakes than she bargained for.

**Required consequence:** A concrete but provisional institutional arrangement — for example, an interim joint-oversight arrangement between two Clans, or a new "unresolved status" administrative category — a precedent and a changed range of imaginable alternatives, not a settled legal outcome.

**State exiting:** Committed, not heroic — she has lost the possibility of returning to who she was, and gained the start of her own understanding of the world in its place. No resolved political status, no Karasu answer, no departure from Yamashiro yet.
