## Core Cast

### Kang Seorin: Haejin Protagonist

**Status:** 🟢 Approved foundation.

**Age and rank:** Thirty; A-rank Sovereign Arm.

**Position:** Contracted extraction Hunter and public face of **Munyeong Extraction**, an aggressive Deep Ports guild that grew from one leased recovery vessel into a Compact contender during Seorin's sixteen years of service.

**Background:** Seorin grew up around the repair slips of Guryong Harbor. Her mother kept accounts for two dock crews; her father repaired submerged pilings and died of lung damage before Seorin's Marking. At fourteen, Seorin was caught in a sea-Aperture failure beneath a municipal loading pier. Her first ability use removed an Ura mass that had fused into the pier's foundation. She freed trapped workers and caused a secondary collapse that destroyed two slips. Munyeong sponsored her, paid the claims against her family, and secured her legal standing when larger guilds refused the risk.

**Public identity:** Haejin traders call her the cleanest extractor alive. Munyeong places her face on contract notices, investor reports, and recruitment broadsheets. Her presence changes salvage bids, fleet schedules, and insurance terms.

**Private condition:** Munyeong owns her license. Its fleet loans and Compact vote rely on her projected yield. Her schedule, public appearances, recovery periods, and outside work require guild approval.

**Personal want:** Purchase her license and establish a small independent extraction operation whose jobs she can choose.

**Need:** Claim responsibility as part of self-ownership. Seorin must choose her obligations rather than define freedom as the absence of restraint.

**Core flaw:** She assumes each relationship will reveal a price. She tests loyalty by becoming difficult to support, then treats departure as proof that her suspicion was correct.

**Competence beyond power:**

- Reads ship loading, harbor construction, underwater failure, and extraction economics.
- Commands mixed Hunter and civilian crews during dangerous recovery work.
- Understands bids, insurance exclusions, salvage shares, and Compact procedure.
- Performs public confidence for clients without surrendering operational control.
- Remembers the load path of structures she has worked on and the names of workers injured under her command.

**Blind spots:**

- Treats requests for mutual dependence as attempts at ownership.
- Overestimates how well other people understand the risks she sees.
- Believes she can absorb the cost of retaliation alone even when the port carries it with her.
- Knows how to price damage and uses that skill to avoid grieving it.

**Voice:** Commanding, socially fluent, and precise about money. She uses polished guild language in formal rooms and blunt dock speech with her crew. She rarely threatens. In anger, she identifies the Ura-treated parts holding a room, ship, or district together.

**Fear:** That the guild's account of her life is correct: without its contract, she is a dangerous accident with expensive habits and no lawful place to stand.

**Anger:** Controlled until she makes a decision. Once committed, she stops negotiating and begins issuing work instructions.

**Kindness:** Pays attention to recovery time, equipment fit, and whether junior workers understand the risk they accepted. She hides care inside professional standards because affection feels usable against her.

**Grudges:**

- Munyeong used her childhood rescue as recruitment mythology while charging its settlement back through her contract.
- Executives postponed her recovery after a spinal injury to preserve a quarterly extraction target.
- The financier guild classified gifts to her family as advances against future yield.
- Guild counsel used her temper during a closed review as evidence that she required tighter assignment control.
- Her image recruited young sponsored Hunters into terms they could not negotiate.

**Ability classifications:**

- System record: `[ABILITY: SUBSTRATE SEVERANCE]`.
- Munyeong sales name: **Perfect Draw**.
- Seorin's own term: **the pull**.

She perceives and severs bonds between nonliving Ura-derived matter and its substrate. The limits and costs in `ARC_02_PLAN.md` govern every use.

**Relationship to Kasane:** Seorin recognizes Haejin's appraisal of Kasane before Kasane does. She initially treats Kasane as a competent foreign specialist who has not yet understood the price of remaining useful. She respects Kasane's refusal to certify unsafe work and resents her ability to leave Haejin under foreign protection.

**What Seorin gets wrong about Kasane:** She mistakes supervised mobility for freedom and assumes Kasane can withdraw from the operation without Yamashiro consequences.

**What Kasane gets wrong about Seorin:** Kasane reads Seorin's command of guild procedure as evidence that she still believes the system can produce a fair answer. Seorin uses procedure because she wants the system to record its refusal before she acts outside it.

**Arc movement:** Seorin begins prepared to purchase private freedom. Munyeong reveals that it has pledged her future yield. She refuses unsafe work, destroys part of Guryong Harbor to rescue a replacement crew, continues into retaliation, and accepts that the retaliatory destruction belongs to her. She rejects a private settlement, protects her crew from scapegoating, and wins a narrow severance ruling. She ends with ownership of her license, large liabilities, and a small operation governed by obligations she chose.

### Yun Myeongha: Senior Underwriter and Primary Antagonist

**Status:** 🟡 Working development.

**Age:** Fifty-eight.

**Position:** Senior underwriter in the **Saerim Ledger Guild**, a financier guild whose loans support several Deep Ports extraction houses. She approved Munyeong's sponsorship of Seorin and built her reputation on finding profitable capability where conservative financiers saw unacceptable risk.

**Independent life before the arc:** Myeongha is defending Saerim's Deep Ports portfolio against an Old Pearl Coast faction that wants to end risky youth sponsorships and concentrate capital in proven bloodlines and established houses. Seorin stands as Myeongha's strongest evidence that backing poor, unknown Marked children can work.

**Competence:**

- Understands linked obligations across fleets, licenses, insurers, and Compact votes.
- Remembers the human histories behind accounts and uses that knowledge in negotiation.
- Can offer terms that solve a person's immediate problem while binding their future.
- Predicts how rival guilds will profit from a default before the debtor sees the danger.

**Belief:** Sponsors finance many failures for each success. A successful Hunter repays the system that gave unproven children equipment, training, standing, and a path out of poverty. Release at the point of profitability transfers all early risk to the sponsor and all later benefit to the individual.

**Personal stake:** If Seorin leaves under the original clause, Munyeong may default and Myeongha's sponsorship model may lose Saerim's internal vote. Future capital will move toward wealthy families and established guilds. She believes Seorin's private freedom could close the door that admitted her.

**Relationship to Seorin:** Patron, evaluator, and owner in practice. Myeongha visited Seorin during recovery, fought for her training budget, and protected her from executives who wanted a faster return. She also approved each valuation method that kept Seorin bound.

**What Myeongha loves about Seorin:** Her discipline, appetite for work, and refusal to treat poverty as a reason for small ambition.

**What Myeongha cannot accept:** Seorin believes the opportunity created a debt with an endpoint. Myeongha believes the returns must sustain the institution that creates the next opportunity.

**Voice:** Warm, exact, and patient. She names amounts and people from memory. She does not use ownership language. She speaks of continuity, exposure, and the workers whose wages rest on a functioning account.

**Authority limit:** Myeongha controls credit terms and valuation. She cannot command Hunters in the field, dictate Compact rulings, or protect Munyeong if its officers falsify safety records in a way rivals can prove.

**Response to the rampage:** Horror, grief, and confirmation. She supports rescue restitution and uses the retaliatory damage to argue that Seorin's contract protected the public. She does not fabricate the destruction or need to exaggerate it.

**End state:** Saerim removes Myeongha from direct supervision of Hunter accounts and she loses her proxy influence over Munyeong's Compact vote. She keeps her guild membership and much of her professional standing. She regards the narrow severance ruling as a costly correction to concealment, not proof that sponsorship itself is wrong.

### Park Doyun: Munyeong Guild Master

**Status:** 🟡 Working development.

**Age:** Forty-nine.

**Position:** Founder and guild master of Munyeong Extraction.

**Independent life before the arc:** Doyun is trying to keep Munyeong's newly expanded fleet solvent through the annual Compact reassessment. The expansion could make Munyeong the first Deep Ports extraction house to displace an Old Pearl Coast seat. A failed quarter would leave its ships, worker housing, and licenses open to acquisition.

**Competence:**

- Built a functioning extraction guild from one recovery vessel and a rented yard.
- Knows crews, captains, and repair schedules rather than managing only through reports.
- Can coordinate high-risk operations across civilian and Hunter teams.
- Earns loyalty by sharing hardship and remembering who did the work.

**Blind spot:** Doyun believes shared hardship gives him a moral claim on Seorin's continued service. He sees Munyeong as something they built together and treats her departure as abandonment of workers whose livelihoods grew around her.

**Relationship to Seorin:** He recruited her at fourteen and taught her the operational side of extraction. He is proud of her, afraid of her, and dependent on her. He has delayed her buyout conversations for years while telling himself the next profitable season would make release possible.

**Voice:** Practical, persuasive, and impatient with financial abstraction unless he needs it. He speaks to Seorin as an old crew member in private and as a guild asset in rooms where lenders can hear him.

**Authority limit:** Doyun assigns Munyeong crews and controls its records. Saerim can call his loans, the Compact can strip operating rights, and Seorin can refuse to move.

**Response to the rampage:** He orders evacuation during the rescue stage. During retaliation, he tries to save Munyeong's fleet and records. He later attempts to place responsibility on field-level concealment rather than accept that he authorized the operation.

**Arc movement:** Doyun begins treating Seorin's release as an unaffordable delay. He ends without her, with a smaller guild or no guild at all, forced to face which parts of Munyeong were an institution and which parts were one woman's contracted labor.

### Im Nari: Operations Chief

**Status:** 🟡 Working development.

**Age:** Thirty-six.

**Rank:** Unmarked civilian.

**Position:** Munyeong's senior field operations chief. Nari turns Hunter capability into workable extraction plans and holds stop-work authority on paper.

**Independent life before the arc:** Nari has been building enough personal credit to join Seorin's proposed independent operation. She also supports a widowed brother and two nieces through Munyeong housing, which she would lose if the guild dismissed her.

**Competence:**

- Plans tides, diving rotations, load paths, evacuation windows, and civilian labor.
- Knows when a powerful Hunter's confidence exceeds the crew's margin.
- Can stop a deck through command presence without rank or Marking.
- Maintains private copies of safety amendments because official ledgers have changed after accidents before.

**Blind spot:** Nari believes preparation can keep her morally clear inside a compromised institution. She documents bad decisions and still helps execute many of them.

**Relationship to Seorin:** Twelve years of field trust. Nari intends to leave with Seorin but refuses worship and will countermand her when workers face avoidable danger.

**Core disagreement:** Seorin thinks Nari's loyalty should survive the worst version of her. Nari believes loyalty requires opposition when Seorin becomes the danger.

**Voice:** Concrete and unsentimental. She uses names, distances, and times. Anger makes her formal.

**Authority limit:** She can halt civilian work under Munyeong procedure. Doyun can replace her, and a guild Hunter can override her during a declared Aperture emergency.

**Role in the evidence chain:** Nari retains the original survey appendix showing synchronized stress between the Aperture boundary and the harbor's Ura reinforcement. Her copy proves that Munyeong removed the warning from the packet sent to Yamashiro.

**Response to the rampage:** Nari directs evacuation and helps Seorin reach the trapped team. Once rescue access exists, she orders Seorin to stop. Seorin refuses. Nari joins the independent operation only after Seorin accepts liability and grants crew stop-work authority that applies to the owner as well.

### Baek Juna: Younger Sponsored Hunter

**Status:** 🟡 Working development.

**Age:** Nineteen.

**Rank:** D-rank Contracted.

**Position:** Junior Munyeong Hunter assigned to material containment and dive-team protection.

**Independent life before the arc:** Juna sends most of her pay to parents on a Salt Island whose fishing income collapsed after a route change. She wants promotion before their housing note comes due. Munyeong's sponsorship gave her legal work when no island guild would fund her equipment.

**Competence:**

- Maintains a short-lived pressure shell around people and fragile recovered material.
- Reads crew fear and keeps divers moving during bad visibility.
- Learns procedures fast and asks questions senior workers stopped asking.

**Blind spot:** Juna interprets Seorin's fame as proof that hard work earns leverage. She sees warnings about sponsorship as bitterness from people who failed to rise.

**Relationship to Seorin:** Admiration mixed with professional hunger. Munyeong uses Seorin to recruit her generation. Seorin recognizes how much of her own public performance Juna has mistaken for a life.

**Voice:** Fast, prepared, and eager to use correct guild terminology. Fear makes her repeat procedure.

**Authority limit:** Juna can protect a small team for seconds. She cannot refuse a lawful assignment without risking her license, family remittances, and promotion.

**Role in the failed operation:** After Seorin refuses deployment, Munyeong assigns Juna's team to demonstrate that the site does not depend on one Hunter. Juna enters with incomplete safety findings. Her shell keeps several workers alive after the collapse and cannot create an exit.

**Response to the rampage:** Seorin rescues her. Juna sees the retaliation from inside the damage and cannot reduce Seorin to either hero or monster afterward.

**Arc movement:** Juna begins defending sponsorship through Seorin's example. She ends with direct knowledge of both the system's coercion and the harm Seorin caused. She does not join Seorin's new operation. She remains contracted and files one of the severance petitions that later fails.

### Kang Mira: Seorin's Older Sister

**Status:** 🟡 Working development.

**Age:** Thirty-eight.

**Position:** Co-owner and working manager of a repair yard in Guryong Harbor. Munyeong contracts supply much of the yard's work.

**Independent life before the arc:** Mira is negotiating to buy the adjoining slip before an Old Pearl Coast company acquires it. Her employees include relatives of workers whom Seorin rescued during her first manifestation.

**Competence:**

- Estimates repair labor and material loss from a glance at a damaged hull.
- Maintains relationships across crews that refuse to speak to guild management.
- Knows which parts of Seorin's public story Munyeong omitted because she lived through the original accident.

**Belief:** Munyeong saved their family from claims, poverty, and the possibility that authorities would classify fourteen-year-old Seorin as an uncontrolled public danger. Gratitude does not erase exploitation, but Mira refuses to rewrite rescue as pure malice.

**Relationship to Seorin:** Loving, strained, and financially entangled. Seorin paid for the yard's expansion through wages that the guild now cites as evidence of generous treatment. Mira wants Seorin free and fears what freedom will do to the family businesses built around Munyeong.

**Voice:** Familiar, impatient, and immune to Seorin's public manner. She argues through remembered facts rather than principles.

**Authority limit:** Mira has no standing over Seorin's license. Her yard depends on contracts that Doyun can withdraw.

**Response to the rampage:** The retaliation damages Mira's yard after the rescue corridor has opened. She shelters displaced workers and refuses to let either the guild or Seorin describe the loss as an abstraction.

**Arc movement:** Mira begins asking Seorin to delay the buyout until the yard can survive it. She ends supporting Seorin's public disclosure while demanding restitution from her new operation.

## Secondary Antagonist and Institutional Cast

### Seo Gwanil: Old Pearl Coast Guild Master

**Status:** 🟡 Working development.

**Age:** Sixty-one.

**Position:** Master of **White Current Recovery**, an old Pearl Coast extraction house with inherited Compact influence.

**Independent life before the arc:** Gwanil wants Munyeong's Compact seat, vessels, and harbor rights. He opposes Myeongha's youth-sponsorship model because it creates volatile competitors and weakens old-house control of certification.

**Competence:** Patient acquisition. He lets rivals assume he seeks Seorin when his preferred outcome is Munyeong's default and division into assets he can purchase.

**Relationship to Seorin:** Offers to acquire her contract under better terms. He treats improved conditions as proof that ownership itself is not the problem.

**Voice:** Courteous and historical. He frames current extraction rights as obligations his house has maintained for generations.

**Authority limit:** Gwanil cannot force a sale before default or control what Seorin does under Munyeong command. He relies on lenders, Compact procedure, and time.

**Response to the rampage:** White Current supplies emergency vessels, earns public credit, and buys damaged Munyeong assets at reduced prices. Gwanil benefits regardless of Seorin's legal outcome.

### Chae Hojin: Compact Claims Examiner

**Status:** 🟡 Working development.

**Age:** Forty-three.

**Position:** Claims examiner assigned to cross-guild Aperture disputes and the later severance hearing.

**Independent life before the arc:** Hojin is seeking renewal to a rotating Compact office. A ruling that frightens financiers could end his appointment; a ruling that ignores documented fraud could make him the fall person when the evidence spreads.

**Competence:** Separates operational fact, contract language, and political framing. He knows a narrow ruling can change practice without announcing a new principle.

**Blind spot:** Treats procedural access as fairness even when one party can afford years of delay and another cannot.

**Relationship to Seorin:** No prior personal loyalty. He respects the quality of her operational testimony and distrusts her attempt to treat damage as a debt she can price alone.

**Voice:** Spare, literal, and resistant to speeches. He asks who signed, who knew, and which rule supplied authority.

**Authority limit:** Hojin recommends findings and drafts the severance terms. Compact members approve the ruling and may narrow it.

**Arc movement:** He begins trying to contain a politically dangerous claim. The evidence forces him to write a misconduct standard that other Hunters can cite, even after the Compact narrows its use.

## Yamashiro Carryovers

### Kasane

**Status entering Arc 2:** Twenty-seven; E-rank Traced; eight months of supervised field development under Isato. She uses the Stitch with more precision and less hesitation inside the Five Limits. Increased competence has not removed cost or granted a new function.

**Independent pressure:** Haejin's request offers field knowledge while exposing her to foreign ownership claims. Shirogane can use any breach of the travel terms to tighten oversight. Kuromori expects useful data and conduct it can defend.

**Function in Seorin's story:** Technical witness, outsider, and partial restraint during the harbor collapse. She can stabilize isolated failures and verify boundary behavior. She cannot free Seorin, govern Haejin crews, absolve the rampage, or choose the settlement.

**Relationship movement with Seorin:** Mutual appraisal develops into respect without easy agreement. Seorin teaches Kasane how institutions can price protection. Kasane forces Seorin to confront the difference between chosen restraint and imposed restraint by refusing to claim moral authority over her.

### Isato

**Status entering Arc 2:** Kasane's primary handler of record. His standing now depends on whether her supervised development supports Kuromori's judgment.

**Independent pressure:** He must protect Kasane, respect Haejin field command, collect useful evidence, and prevent Shirogane from receiving grounds to end the arrangement. These duties conflict during the failed operation and rampage.

**Competence:** Conventional Gate command, evacuation, threat assessment, and the ability to say no to Kasane without treating caution as weakness.

**Authority limit:** He directs Kasane under Yamashiro's arrangement. He has no command over Seorin, Munyeong, or Haejin civilians.

**Relationship movement:** Isato sees Kasane operate beyond Yamashiro and must decide whether handling her means enforcing every term or defending her judgment when the terms no longer fit the danger.

### Noe

**Status entering Arc 2:** Still E-rank Traced, still living with the material consequences of stalled advancement. Her relationship with Kasane has developed during the eight-month gap without resolving every uncertainty.

**Direct appearance:** One departure scene with its own decision. Noe refuses a benefit offered through Kasane's growing prominence if accepting it would make their relationship part of Kasane's institutional account. Kasane must decide whether respecting that refusal means withholding help or finding a form Noe can accept.

**Off-page continuation:** Their correspondence remains personal and uneven. Use a message only when it affects Kasane's judgment in Haejin.

## Concealed Safety Evidence

Munyeong's first survey recorded synchronized stress pulses between the unstable Aperture boundary and the Ura-treated reinforcement running through the harbor sector. A full extraction attempt could propagate failure from the site into cranes, pilings, and seawalls.

Doyun authorized removal of the appendix from the technical packet sent to Yamashiro because Saerim would freeze the operation if foreign review confirmed it. Myeongha received a summary warning and approved continued assessment, expecting Seorin's precision and Kasane's stabilization to reduce the risk before full deployment.

Nari retained the original appendix with survey signatures and time records. Kasane's observations independently reproduce its central finding. Together, the documents and testimony establish concealment without giving any character knowledge of the Erosion or the Tideless Mile's cause.

## Rampage Consequence Calibration

The outline must assign each casualty to a cause and keep the count stable once approved.

🟡 Working calibration:

- The failed replacement operation kills several workers before Seorin opens the rescue corridor.
- Seorin's rescue-stage destruction prevents a much larger loss.
- Her retaliatory continuation causes additional deaths or irreversible injuries among people who were not responsible for the concealed risk.
- The retaliation destroys homes, small businesses, and Mira's repair yard alongside Munyeong assets.
- Seorin never targets civilians as targets. Her decision to continue while knowing collapse has widened makes the harm hers.

## Cast Tests

### Independent-life test

- Myeongha fights an internal capital dispute without Kasane.
- Doyun risks Munyeong's fleet and Compact position without Kasane.
- Nari plans her exit while supporting family in guild housing.
- Juna pursues promotion to protect her parents.
- Mira tries to acquire a repair slip and keep her workers employed.
- Gwanil seeks Munyeong's assets and seat.
- Hojin seeks renewal and a defensible claims record.

### Authority-limit test

No character can solve the arc through position alone. Myeongha controls valuation but not field command. Doyun commands Munyeong but not its credit. Nari can stop civilian work but can be overridden. Seorin can destroy infrastructure but cannot grant herself legal standing. Kasane can verify boundary behavior but cannot decide Haejin ownership law. Hojin can draft a ruling but cannot approve it.

### Protagonist-distinction test

Seorin combines an infrastructure-dependent power, commercial literacy, public asset status, crew command, and contract ownership. Her methods and conflict fail outside Haejin's ports, financier guilds, and Ura-material economy.

### Relationship-pressure test

The rampage changes each central relationship differently. Nari opposes Seorin and later joins under enforceable stop-work terms. Juna survives and refuses to join. Mira supports disclosure and demands restitution. Myeongha loses control without changing her core belief. Doyun loses the institution he treated as shared ownership.
