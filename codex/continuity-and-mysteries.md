## AUTHOR-ONLY — LATE-SAGA REVEAL — DO NOT LEAK INTO EARLY DRAFTING

**Status:** AUTHOR-ONLY

**What the world knows:** Nothing. In-world, the Reflection Hypothesis, the Older World Hypothesis, the Wound Hypothesis, the Divine-Revelation doctrine, and the Null Hypothesis (see `Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — The Ura: Ontology and Competing Theories) continue to compete exactly as written, with no character aware of a "correct" answer among them.

**What specific characters may know:** No character currently has access to this framing. Not Kasane. Not any institution.

**What the reader may infer:** Nothing early. Late-saga, the reader may begin to sense that the map — literal and conceptual — was only ever showing one side of something larger (see `Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — Section 13.11, "The Golden Rule of the Map").

**Author truth, if established:**
- 🟢 **Adopted late-saga direction (confirmed):** the human/Ura distinction is itself an artificial framework — "world," "Ura," "Gate," "boundary," and "distance" are human categories imposed on something that doesn't actually divide that way. Kasane's Stitch works not because she reaches *across* a fixed boundary but because the boundary has stopped treating her as being entirely on one side. This is now the intended endpoint, not one candidate among several — but it stays a **late-stage reveal**: none of Sections 5B's competing theories should be rewritten to foreshadow it early, no character currently has access to this framing, and the Reflection/Older World/Wound Hypothesis debate continues to play out in-world exactly as written until the saga is ready to move past it. Adopting the answer doesn't mean spoiling it.
- 🟢 **Critical guardrail on that reveal (extends Section 11's Erosion material):** discovering that humanity's cosmology was incomplete must never retroactively mean **the Erosion was right all along**. Even if the Erosion turns out to be restoring an older state, eliminating an artificial division, or following some natural tendency, the human cost of total breach stays real — and humanity's desire to preserve its world doesn't prove the existing arrangement was correct either. The endgame should resist collapsing into "preserve the false world = good" vs. "restore the true world = good." A process can be natural and still catastrophic; a boundary can be artificial and still worth preserving. Kasane's whole skillset — working with a dangerous boundary rather than treating it as strictly open-or-closed — is the thematic model for refusing that binary (Section 11.14).

- 🟢 Adopted late-saga direction (confirmed, see Section 5H): the Stitch doesn't cross a fixed boundary at all — the boundary itself has stopped recognizing Kasane as being entirely on one side. This is now the intended eventual reveal, held back for late-saga — the Stitch's mechanics as written in this section (a personal, momentary suspension of the matter-interaction rule) remain the correct *working* model for all drafting until the saga is actually ready to move past it.

**Earliest acceptable reveal:** Late saga. Not before. Sections 5B's competing theories must not be rewritten to foreshadow it, and no early chapter may leak this framing even obliquely.

**Do not accidentally reveal:** That "human," "Ura," "Gate," "boundary," and "distance" are categories rather than fixed facts; that the Stitch works by the boundary no longer treating Kasane as entirely on one side (rather than her "crossing" it); and — critically — do not let the reveal collapse into "the Erosion was right all along." The human cost of total breach stays real regardless of what the reveal turns out to mean.

## The Erosion's True Nature

**Status:** ⚪

**What the world knows:** Gate frequency and severity are increasing; no region has the full picture; each region treats only its own symptoms (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — The Erosion, Institutions).

**What specific characters may know:** Not yet established. Karasu's leadership does not even know whether their own clan's silent Gate was a successful interruption of the Erosion, a coincidence, or something worse.

**What the reader may infer:** That escalation happens in surges tied to real historical catastrophes (Akaishi's territory-destroying Gate surge; Erkhaan's founding-war Hundred-Year Legion loss — both retroactively framed, 🟢, as Erosion surge events) — before any character connects them.

**Author truth, if established:** Not yet established. Explicitly linked to, and must not be resolved independently of, the unresolved ontological questions in Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md (whether the Ura has one coherent nature; Wound Hypothesis vs. Older World Hypothesis).

**Earliest acceptable reveal:** Not yet established — mid-to-late saga at the earliest, per the map-reveal pacing (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — Section 13.11) and the Erosion's own narrative-visibility pacing (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — The Erosion, deep expansion).

**Do not accidentally reveal:** Why the Erosion selects the specific weak points it does. Whether it is a symptom of the Ura's nature or an external actor exploiting a weakness in an otherwise-stable Older World — these two are incompatible and the saga should not need to pick one prematurely.

## The Karasu Silence

**Status:** ⚪ (three simultaneously live interpretive threads, deliberately unresolved)

**What the world knows:** Karasu's clan seat sits abandoned at the edge of a silent Gate zone (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — Yamashiro). It is watched, not lived in.

**What specific characters may know:** Not yet established. Karasu's own leadership does not know which of the three threads below is true.

**What the reader may infer:** That the Karasu Silence is simultaneously (1) a bad omen per Yamashiro folk belief, (2) a "Silent"-category Gate under the Section 9 taxonomy (closed unexpectedly, without full resolution, leaving an ambiguous aftermath), and (3) a possible successful Erosion-interruption — meaning interruption, not necessarily defeat, may be a real and demonstrated capability.

**Author truth, if established:** Not yet established. All three threads should stay simultaneously plausible until the story is ready to commit to one.

**Earliest acceptable reveal:** A strong candidate for a Yamashiro arc climax or a later convergence beat — not before.

**Do not accidentally reveal:** Whether Karasu's silence was a successful interruption, a coincidence, or something worse.

## The Weeping Twin

**Status:** ⚪ (myth fragment — deliberately contradictory across oral variants)

**What the world knows:** She was kin to the Boundary Kami, opposed the opening, was silenced, and something happened to her at the boundary. Variants disagree on whether she was sealed beyond it, became the first creature through, still cries whenever a Gate forms, was killed by the Boundary Kami, or willingly took the first wound into herself. Told by priests attached to a minor, tolerated cult (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — Rumor, Myth, and Information).

**What specific characters may know:** Not yet established.

**What the reader may infer:** That the myth's contradictions are the point, not a gap to be filled — a suspiciously clean single-version myth would itself be a tell under the world's own rumor-evaluation lens. Careful readers may notice the myth maps unusually well onto the Wound Hypothesis.

**Author truth, if established:** Not yet established. The proximity to the Wound Hypothesis is intentional resonance, not coincidence, and must not be accidentally contradicted.

**Earliest acceptable reveal:** Not yet established.

**Do not accidentally reveal:** Any single "true" version of the myth — the contradiction itself is load-bearing.

**Status:** ⚪ (suppressed heresy)

**What the world knows:** Officially labeled heresy by the Bailian Communion; the Forbidden Provinces are access-restricted partly to protect this doctrine from inconvenient evidence (`Bible/01_WORLD_BIBLE.md` — The Other Four Regions, 13.2 Bailian).

**What specific characters may know:** Told by lower clergy against their own institution's interest — the strongest corroborating signal available in-world, since people rarely fabricate stories that get them in trouble with their own superiors.

**What the reader may infer:** A possible link to the unconfirmed "Gates within Gates" report, which surfaces exactly once in Bailian's suppressed record.

**Author truth, if established:** Not yet established. Whether the Forbidden Provinces contain direct evidence relevant to the First Rent or the Erosion's nature is deliberately unconfirmed.

**Earliest acceptable reveal:** Not yet established — a reformist Thousand Monasteries scholar-monk investigating this account is flagged as a narrative opportunity, not a scheduled reveal.

**Do not accidentally reveal:** Whether the Hollow Monk's account is actually true.

## Additional Open Questions (catalogue, one line each)

- ⚪ Whether the Ura has a single coherent nature at all, or is fundamentally plural/inconsistent in a way that makes every competing theory partially true and partially a category error.
- ⚪ Whether genuine non-human intelligence exists natively in the Ura, and whether the Erosion is part of this ecology or categorically separate from it.
- ⚪ Whether reversal of Kasane's Marking is truly impossible, or simply never attempted by anyone who lived to report success — and whether her dual-eye anomaly has ever occurred before, anywhere, and gone unrecorded.
- ⚪ Whether the Stitch can influence existing Gates — deliberately left untested — and what, precisely, the Erosion's "interest" in Stitch use actually means or wants.
- ⚪ Why Sustained Gates specifically resist sealing.
- ⚪ Whether any Haejin guild leadership actually understands the Erosion's role in Gate escalation, or genuinely believes the frequency increase is a natural resource boom to be exploited.
- ⚪ Whether Erkhaan's black-program interest in Gate weaponization has any connection to central command officially, or is a rogue faction operating with plausible deniability.
- ⚪ Whether Deep Harrow communities have encountered Ura phenomena no other region has ever documented.
- ⚪ What SS-rank capability would actually require, and whether the last Shinken came anywhere close before dying; whether any institution has ever deliberately suppressed a promotion to keep a dangerously capable person off their own books.
- ⚪ Whether a formal cross-regional response to the Erosion ever comes into being, and if so, which region's institution bends first.
- ❓ Development question: whether the universal rank notation (F–SS) was ever deliberately designed by some earlier cross-regional actor now lost to history, or whether all five regions converged on it independently — left as background texture, not a mystery the saga needs to resolve.
- ⚪ Whether anyone currently alive has independently assembled all four (or more) myth fragments into a single coherent picture, and whether such a person would be believed by anyone with the institutional power to act on it.
- ❓ Development question: whether any region's theory of the Ura is "closer" to the adopted late-saga truth than the others — no character or reader is meant to hold more synthesis than any character ever does; needs an explicit author decision before it affects later arcs.
