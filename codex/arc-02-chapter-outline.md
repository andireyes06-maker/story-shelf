# ARC 2: HAEJIN: CHAPTER OUTLINE

**Status:** Approved outline built from the approved plan and cast. Chapter prompts may refine scene execution but must not change the causal sequence without author review.

**Planned length:** 22 chapters across eight movements. The sequence uses three chapters before Kasane arrives, seven from her arrival through the joint refusal, six for coercion through the rampage, five for aftermath through adjudication, and one closing chapter. The count does not copy Arc 1's nineteen-chapter shape.

**POV distribution:** Seorin owns 15 chapters, Kasane owns 5, Juna owns 1, and Nari owns 1. Each scene keeps one POV. No chapter changes POV unless its prompt later marks a scene break and a specific reason.

## Decisions Fixed for Sequencing

### Guryong Harbor geography

Guryong Harbor sits inside a hooked natural inlet in the Deep Ports.

- **Outer Teeth:** two staggered seawalls built with Ura-treated reinforcement. They break storm force before it reaches the working basin.
- **Munyeong Basin:** the sponsor guild's leased water, three extraction berths, recovery sheds, and fleet moorings.
- **Seven Crane Row:** heavy lift cranes and sorting platforms shared by several guilds. Ura-treated anchor rails connect the row to the basin foundations.
- **Low Slips:** repair yards, boarding houses, food stalls, and worker homes along the inner water. Mira's yard occupies the fourth slip.
- **Tidehouse:** Munyeong's offices, contract archive, clinic, and public receiving hall on raised ground behind the basin.
- **Dry Channel:** an old flood-cut used as an emergency drainage and evacuation route. Later construction narrowed it and placed worker housing near its inland mouth.
- **Survey Site K-17:** the unstable sea-Aperture below the basin's eastern approach. Ura reinforcement links nearby pilings, crane rails, and part of the eastern Outer Tooth through decades of repair.

The synchronized stress pulse at K-17 can travel through this reinforcement. Seorin knows the network because Munyeong used her recovered material in several repairs.

### Casualty and damage ledger

Keep these numbers stable unless the author approves a revision before drafting:

- The failed replacement operation traps thirty-eight people.
- Seven die before Seorin opens the rescue route.
- Rescue-stage collapse kills two more while Seorin and the crews save twenty-nine.
- Seorin's retaliatory continuation kills four people and causes nineteen life-changing injuries.
- The harbor authorities displace 312 Low Slips residents after structural inspection.
- Munyeong loses two vessels, its eastern recovery shed, most heavy extraction machinery, and access to one berth.
- Seven Crane Row loses three cranes and remains at half capacity at the arc's end.
- Mira's fourth-slip yard becomes unusable.
- The eastern Outer Tooth remains breached. Temporary barriers alter currents and shipping windows through the final chapter.

Seorin never selects civilians as targets. She continues after Nari warns her that the collapse path has reached occupied structures. The four retaliatory deaths therefore belong to her decision.

### Tideless Mile connection

The K-17 survey contains a repeated interval with no expected tidal lag. Fishing crews use the same practical description for crossings near the Tideless Mile: current changes arrive without the delay sailors expect from distance and depth.

The harbor collapse exposes an older Ura deposit beneath the eastern approach. Its layers show no directional wear from tide or current. Kasane and local workers can identify the pattern. They cannot explain its cause. Myeongha and Doyun treated the no-lag readings as survey noise because accepting them would freeze the site.

The arc carries this evidence as a labor and safety problem. No character identifies the Erosion or learns the Mile's author-only truth.

### Kasane's travel restriction

Shirogane's travel instrument permits Stitch use at the designated K-17 worksite under Isato's direct authorization. Emergency use elsewhere requires an immediate threat to life and Isato's confirmation where circumstances allow.

During the rampage, debris separates Kasane from Isato. She uses the Stitch in Low Slips housing outside K-17 and without his confirmation to stabilize a failing Ura-treated foundation long enough for evacuation. The act fits the life-safety exception and violates the direct-authorization reading Moritaka insisted upon. Aya and Isato later defend the decision; Moritaka cites it as grounds for review.

### Noe departure decision

Kuromori offers Noe paid work as Kasane's home liaison: maintain a contact address, report changes in Kasane's ordinary routines, and help staff understand when training has affected her. The role would clear Noe's housing debt and improve her rank file.

Noe refuses because the office would turn their personal relationship into oversight infrastructure. Kasane first tries to solve the material problem by offering money. Noe refuses that form as well. Kasane then asks Noe to keep writing with no report attached and states that she wants the relationship to continue for her own sake. Noe accepts. The scene advances the romance through consent and boundary-setting without resolving it.

### First independent operation

Seorin's new operation accepts a low-margin Salt Islands recovery contract that larger guilds declined. A storm sank medical cargo near a damaged island route; the island can pay a fixed salvage share but cannot cover a premium Hunter rate. Seorin accepts under the crew terms established after the hearing.

The job does not redeem the rampage or prove the new operation will survive. It shows Seorin choosing work under terms she owns.

## Movement I: The Price That Moved

### Chapter 1: The Cleanest Cut

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Establish Seorin's power, work, fame, crew command, and Haejin's ordinary contract economy before Kasane enters the arc.

**Immediate want:** Complete a difficult extraction ahead of the tide window and preserve the yield needed for her buyout.

**Scene problem:** Munyeong has accepted overlapping salvage promises to a ship owner and a material buyer. Seorin can recover the Ura core intact or free the trapped civilian hull before the tide turns; the schedule does not permit both in the order Doyun sold.

**Choice:** Seorin changes the operation, saves the hull first, and uses the pull at greater physical cost to recover the core after it shifts.

**Information gate:** Show rank through crew behavior and market response. Explain only the parts of sponsorship that workers use during the job. Do not present the full buyout dispute yet.

**Relationship movement:** Nari challenges Doyun's schedule and Seorin backs her in the field. Juna sees Seorin absorb pain without showing the client.

**Required consequence:** Seorin earns the last amount listed in her buyout calculation and aggravates an old joint injury.

**Closing turn:** She submits the buyout notice before leaving the harbor clinic.

### Chapter 2: Future Yield

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Put the central refusal on-page and establish Myeongha's sincere position.

**Immediate want:** Receive the license transfer date promised by the buyout clause.

**Scene problem:** Myeongha and Doyun present a new valuation. Munyeong pledged Seorin's projected output against fleet loans and its Compact reassessment bid. The old amount covers neither replacement cost nor the loss attached to release.

**Choice:** Seorin refuses improved pay, a profit share, and a ceremonial management title. She demands the account and the authority behind the revaluation.

**Information gate:** Reveal the linked obligations. Hold back the concealed K-17 survey and any planned foreign request.

**Relationship movement:** Myeongha speaks as the person who backed Seorin at fourteen. Doyun frames Munyeong as work they built together. Seorin hears affection and ownership in the same room.

**Required consequence:** Saerim starts a formal reassessment. The process freezes Seorin's withdrawal and permits contract transfer if Munyeong misses its obligations.

**Closing turn:** White Current files notice of interest in the contract and Munyeong's eastern berth.

### Chapter 3: What the Guild Saved

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Establish Seorin's family and crew relationships before the external plot arrives.

**Immediate want:** Secure Nari's commitment to the independent operation and learn whether Mira can withstand Munyeong retaliation.

**Scene problem:** Nari's family housing depends on Munyeong. Mira needs one more season of guild repair work to purchase the adjoining slip. Both women support Seorin's freedom and cannot absorb the same timetable.

**Choice:** Seorin offers to cover both risks. Nari and Mira reject her attempt to turn loyalty into debts she can pay.

**Information gate:** Reveal the original accident through disagreement and physical remnants at Mira's yard. Do not summarize Seorin's childhood.

**Relationship movement:** Mira states that Munyeong saved them and has exploited that rescue since. Nari agrees to plan for departure but reserves the right to stop Seorin in the field.

**Required consequence:** Seorin begins seeking leverage outside the buyout process rather than waiting for reassessment.

**Closing turn:** Doyun summons her to discuss a foreign specialist and a site that could secure Munyeong's year.

## Movement II: A Foreign Asset

**POV:** Kasane.

**Chapter job:** Show eight months of Yamashiro development, advance Noe, and establish the restrictions on Kasane's Haejin work.

**Immediate want:** Leave for Haejin with her ordinary relationships clear and the assignment's limits understood.

**Scene problem:** Kuromori offers Noe the paid liaison role. Noe refuses. Kasane's first attempts to help reproduce the problem by assigning material value to the relationship.

**Choice:** Kasane asks Noe to continue writing without any institutional role and says why she wants that contact. She accepts that Noe's housing problem remains unsolved by her.

**Information gate:** Show Kasane's stronger control through a small training or preparation task. State the travel clauses in practical terms. Preserve the unresolved nature of her physical cost.

**Relationship movement:** Noe and Kasane establish a boundary that makes future intimacy possible. Isato demonstrates that he now treats caution as competence.

**Required consequence:** Kasane leaves with personal consent separated from institutional permission.

**Closing turn:** Aya shows her three Haejin valuations of the Rite event, none describing what happened with accuracy.

### Chapter 5: The Unpriced One

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Introduce Kasane from outside and establish Haejin's attempt to appraise her.

**Immediate want:** Determine whether Kasane can make K-17 profitable enough to strengthen Seorin's leverage.

**Scene problem:** Doyun presents a formal welcome while brokers, insurers, and Compact observers seek access. Kasane looks unimpressive to people expecting a public miracle. Seorin sees each party testing what Kasane will permit.

**Choice:** Seorin cuts the reception short and takes Kasane to the working harbor before the financiers can define the relationship.

**Information gate:** Haejin knows conflicting rumors about Kasane. Seorin knows the sponsor request omitted some operational detail but has not seen the original survey appendix.

**Relationship movement:** Seorin respects Kasane's refusal to perform the Stitch for demonstration. Kasane notices that Seorin can end a room without raising her voice.

**Required consequence:** Myeongha treats Seorin's intervention as an assertion of management authority and adds it to the terms she offers later.

**Closing turn:** At K-17, Kasane finds the boundary responding to harbor load rather than only to the sea-Aperture.

### Chapter 6: No Tidal Lag

**POV:** Kasane.

**Chapter job:** Establish K-17's physical problem and the first fair clue toward the Tideless Mile.

**Immediate want:** Determine whether a bounded stabilization test fits the Five Limits and her travel authority.

**Scene problem:** The technical packet predicts a stable delay between tide, structural load, and Aperture response. Kasane observes changes arriving together. Seorin can identify the harbor materials carrying the strain but cannot see the boundary relation Kasane sees.

**Choice:** Kasane permits a contact-range test and refuses the proposed extraction window.

**Information gate:** Reveal the no-lag pattern as measurement. No character connects it to the Erosion. Do not mention the Tideless Mile by name yet.

**Relationship movement:** Seorin sees Kasane stop a profitable operation without performing uncertainty for approval. Kasane sees Seorin accept the halt in public and calculate its cost in private.

**Required consequence:** Munyeong must choose between reporting a compromised site or gathering enough favorable data to keep K-17 open.

**Closing turn:** Nari finds that the packet's appendix numbering skips a section.

## Movement III: Terms of Use

### Chapter 7: Perfect Draw

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Show Seorin and Kasane solving a bounded problem together while their methods remain distinct.

**Immediate want:** Recover a small sample that proves K-17 has value without authorizing full extraction.

**Scene problem:** The sample sits across an unstable material-boundary overlap. Seorin can sever it from the seabed but cannot predict the boundary response. Kasane can hold one contact point and lacks the material knowledge to choose the cut.

**Choice:** They divide authority by task. Seorin controls the cut; Kasane controls the duration of the stabilization and ends it at her limit.

**Information gate:** Demonstrate Kasane's growth through precision and recovery discipline. Preserve the Five Limits. Establish the physical cost of Seorin's pull.

**Relationship movement:** Each woman sees a problem the other can solve. Neither treats the partnership as trust.

**Required consequence:** The sample confirms high value and gives Munyeong a reason to resist closure.

**Closing turn:** The sample contains wear patterns that do not match the direction of any local current.

### Chapter 8: Competing Claims

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Turn the successful sample into legal and commercial pressure.

**Immediate want:** Use her role in the recovery to force a fixed buyout valuation.

**Scene problem:** Munyeong, Saerim, White Current, and Yamashiro assert different rights over the sample, method, and operational record. Myeongha offers Seorin a management share that carries no license ownership.

**Choice:** Seorin demands that Hojin record her personal claim to method and risk findings. Hojin records the claim without deciding it.

**Information gate:** Explain claims through the contested sample. Avoid a general lecture on Compact law.

**Relationship movement:** Gwanil offers Seorin better ownership conditions under a transferred contract. She recognizes the improved cage and refuses.

**Required consequence:** Seorin's contract dispute becomes visible to rival guilds and the Compact.

**Closing turn:** Myeongha tells Seorin that public dispute will raise her replacement cost faster than it raises her leverage.

### Chapter 9: The Missing Appendix

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Move suspicion into evidence and force Nari's compromised position into the open.

**Immediate want:** Find the omitted survey section before Munyeong schedules another test.

**Scene problem:** Nari admits that official accident records have changed before and that she keeps private copies. Producing the appendix could end her employment, housing, and chance to leave with Seorin.

**Choice:** Seorin asks for the copy and promises no protection she cannot provide. Nari shows it but keeps physical custody.

**Information gate:** The appendix records synchronized stress and no tidal lag. It proves omission from the Yamashiro packet but does not yet prove who authorized concealment.

**Relationship movement:** Nari trusts Seorin with evidence and refuses to become another asset Seorin can deploy without consent.

**Required consequence:** Seorin knows full extraction could propagate failure into the harbor network.

**Closing turn:** Doyun issues an extraction order for the annual reassessment window.

### Chapter 10: Refusal in Writing

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Make Seorin's break with Munyeong explicit before the disaster.

**Immediate want:** Stop the K-17 operation and force Doyun to enter his order into the claims record.

**Scene problem:** Doyun argues that losing the window will trigger loan review, layoffs, and White Current acquisition. Myeongha argues that Seorin and Kasane can reduce the risk below the threshold recorded in the appendix.

**Choice:** Seorin refuses deployment and files the refusal. Kasane refuses to certify stabilization conditions. Isato supports her technical limit.

**Information gate:** Myeongha received a summary warning and expected further assessment to resolve it. Hold back Doyun's signed deletion order until the evidence sequence after the rampage.

**Relationship movement:** Doyun calls Seorin's refusal abandonment. Seorin tells Juna that a lawful assignment can still kill her.

**Required consequence:** Munyeong must cancel the operation, override Nari, or prove that K-17 does not depend on Seorin.

**Closing turn:** Doyun removes Nari from field authority and assigns Juna's team to the site.

## Movement IV: Replacement Cost

### Chapter 11: Collateral

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Apply nonphysical coercion and prevent Seorin's refusal from reading as costless.

**Immediate want:** Keep Juna's team out of K-17 without returning under command.

**Scene problem:** Doyun ties cancellation to layoffs and housing loss. Mira's repair agreement and Nari's family quarters enter the review. Juna refuses Seorin's private offer of money and insists she can perform the assignment.

**Choice:** Seorin approaches Hojin for an emergency stay. He lacks authority to halt a licensed operation on a private copy and an unsigned foreign assessment.

**Information gate:** Show how legal access fails through timing and standing. Hojin follows his authority rather than acting as a corrupt obstacle.

**Relationship movement:** Juna accuses Seorin of treating junior Hunters as incapable once they choose differently from her.

**Required consequence:** Seorin exhausts the available lawful stop before the launch.

**Closing turn:** Juna boards the recovery vessel wearing Munyeong's contract insignia modeled on Seorin's.

### Chapter 12: A Lawful Assignment

**POV:** Juna.

**Chapter job:** Show the system and Seorin from the viewpoint of someone who still needs sponsorship to work.

**Immediate want:** Complete the sample recovery and prove that Munyeong's future does not rest on one woman.

**Scene problem:** The team receives a narrowed risk brief. Juna recognizes several precautions from Seorin's warnings and interprets their inclusion as proof that management listened.

**Choice:** Juna continues after the first no-lag reading because procedure still shows margins and withdrawal would end her promotion bid.

**Information gate:** Juna lacks the omitted appendix and Kasane's boundary perception. Give her enough evidence to make the choice understandable.

**Relationship movement:** Juna measures herself against Seorin's public example and repeats instructions Seorin once used during training.

**Required consequence:** The team enters the linked reinforcement zone at peak load.

**Closing turn:** Juna's pressure shell activates around thirty-eight people as the eastern berth begins to move with no preceding wave.

### Chapter 13: Outside the Worksite

**POV:** Kasane.

**Chapter job:** Trigger the disaster, separate Kasane from Isato, and place her travel restriction under real pressure.

**Immediate want:** Reach the Dry Channel and keep the collapse from trapping Low Slips residents.

**Scene problem:** K-17's stress pulse enters Seven Crane Row and the eastern Outer Tooth. Debris cuts Kasane off from Isato. Munyeong delays opening the emergency channel because it would flood stored material and concede the salvage claim.

**Choice:** Kasane uses the Stitch outside the designated worksite to hold a Low Slips foundation long enough for residents to leave. She acts without Isato's confirmation.

**Information gate:** The Stitch stabilizes one local failure. It cannot seal K-17 or hold the harbor network. The no-lag pattern links the failures without explaining them.

**Relationship movement:** Isato, separated from Kasane, chooses to defend her judgment over the strict authorization reading.

**Required consequence:** Residents escape one housing block, Kasane breaches Moritaka's clause, and Juna's team remains trapped.

**Closing turn:** Seorin reaches the eastern approach and starts naming which parts of the harbor she must remove.

## Movement V: What Held the Harbor Together

### Chapter 14: Open the Basin

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Begin the rampage with a necessary rescue decision.

**Immediate want:** Create a route to the thirty-eight trapped workers before Juna's shell fails.

**Scene problem:** The fastest route requires Seorin to remove reinforcement from Munyeong Basin and one section of Seven Crane Row. Doyun orders a slower approach to preserve fleet access and salvage rights.

**Choice:** Seorin rejects Doyun's command and begins the pull. Nari directs the evacuation geometry despite her removal from field authority.

**Information gate:** Track each structural cut and bodily cost. Avoid presenting the harbor as one object Seorin can erase.

**Relationship movement:** Nari and Seorin work with their old field precision. Doyun realizes command has ceased to matter.

**Required consequence:** Seorin opens the route. Seven people have died before access; two die during the rescue collapse; twenty-nine survive.

**Closing turn:** Juna clears the wreckage alive, and Seorin turns toward Munyeong's moored fleet instead of stopping.

### Chapter 15: The Distance Between Cuts

**POV:** Kasane.

**Chapter job:** Show Seorin's retaliation from outside and keep evacuation central while power becomes spectacle.

**Immediate want:** Move civilians beyond the new collapse path and keep local failures from chaining through Low Slips.

**Scene problem:** Seorin removes Ura components from Munyeong vessels, crane rails, and the eastern recovery shed. Each cut changes the path of the next collapse. Kasane cannot predict every path or reach Seorin through the debris.

**Choice:** Kasane spends her remaining safe capacity on occupied structures rather than the guild fleet or K-17 data.

**Information gate:** Kasane sees Seorin choosing targets after rescue has succeeded. Preserve Seorin's interior choice for Chapter 16.

**Relationship movement:** Kasane refuses Isato's attempt to extract her while residents remain inside the failure zone. Isato stays and changes the evacuation plan rather than ordering her again.

**Required consequence:** Kasane limits casualties in one Low Slips section and reaches her physical limit. Seorin's damage spreads past Munyeong property.

**Closing turn:** Nari's warning carries across the basin: the next pull will reach occupied structures and Mira's yard.

### Chapter 16: After Necessity

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Make Seorin own the retaliatory stage and choose its endpoint.

**Immediate want:** Destroy the machinery and records that let Munyeong keep treating people as replaceable inputs.

**Scene problem:** Nari identifies the occupied collapse path. Mira's yard, worker homes, and shared cranes sit inside it. Seorin understands the warning and sees the Tidehouse still standing beyond them.

**Choice:** Seorin continues. She destroys the eastern recovery system and two vessels, then stops short of pulling the Tidehouse foundation when Juna places herself in the path and names the people still inside.

**Information gate:** Seorin remains conscious and capable of choice. Her physical strain distorts sensation without removing agency.

**Relationship movement:** Nari stops assisting after rescue. Juna's admiration breaks. Mira watches her yard fail from the evacuation line.

**Required consequence:** Four people die during retaliation, nineteen suffer life-changing injuries, and the harbor loses homes and shared infrastructure. Seorin collapses from recoil after choosing to stop.

**Closing turn:** As crews carry her away, she hears someone call the contract a seawall that failed.

## Movement VI: The Account After

### Chapter 17: Three Hundred Twelve Beds

**POV:** Nari.

**Chapter job:** Show material aftermath before legal argument takes over.

**Immediate want:** Find beds, medicine, work records, and missing names for displaced Low Slips residents.

**Scene problem:** Munyeong wants Nari's evacuation log for its defense. Hojin needs it for casualty attribution. Seorin asks to know the names attached to the retaliatory deaths. Nari does not trust any of them to use the record without turning people into positions.

**Choice:** Nari gives copies to the families and Hojin, keeps one, and refuses Munyeong exclusive custody.

**Information gate:** Fix the casualty categories and damage map. Show White Current emergency aid arriving with acquisition assessors.

**Relationship movement:** Nari visits Seorin and states that joining the independent operation now depends on authority to stop Seorin as well as any crew member.

**Required consequence:** The public record separates rescue deaths from retaliatory deaths.

**Closing turn:** Mira sends Seorin a repair estimate for the fourth slip with no family discount.

### Chapter 18: The Cost Assigned

**POV:** Seorin.

**Chapter job:** Force Seorin to face people and costs she cannot absorb through one payment.

**Immediate want:** Accept liability without letting Munyeong use her admission to erase the concealed risk.

**Scene problem:** Myeongha offers immediate relief funds if Seorin accepts Saerim's incident framing. Families want answers before hearings. Juna refuses a visit. Mira demands restitution and disclosure in the same conversation.

**Choice:** Seorin signs responsibility for the retaliatory stage and refuses language that merges it with the rescue. She does not claim inability or loss of control.

**Information gate:** Keep legal conclusions open. Establish that Myeongha supports relief while preparing the public-safety argument.

**Relationship movement:** Mira remains her sister and becomes a claimant. Seorin cannot use family loyalty to reduce the debt.

**Required consequence:** Seorin preserves a distinction the Compact must address: Munyeong caused the unsafe operation; Seorin caused additional harm after rescue.

**Closing turn:** Nari brings the original appendix and says its deletion order still exists in Tidehouse's duplicated claims register.

### Chapter 19: The Copy That Survived

**POV:** Kasane.

**Chapter job:** Complete the admissible evidence chain and deepen the Tideless Mile pattern without solving it.

**Immediate want:** Verify that Nari's appendix and the surviving K-17 record describe the same boundary behavior she observed.

**Scene problem:** Tidehouse is unsafe, Saerim claims the duplicate register, and Moritaka's review notice orders Kasane to stop foreign investigative work beyond her assignment.

**Choice:** Kasane limits herself to technical verification while Aya and Hojin establish lawful custody through the cross-regional request. Isato records that he authorized the continuing technical work.

**Information gate:** The records show Doyun approved deletion and Myeongha received the warning summary. The exposed deposit carries no directional wear. A fishing foreman identifies the no-lag description with the Tideless Mile.

**Relationship movement:** Seorin asks Kasane to tell her what the Mile means. Kasane says the pattern is real and refuses to invent the answer Seorin wants.

**Required consequence:** Hojin can admit the concealment evidence. Myeongha can no longer frame K-17 as a mistake based on complete information.

**Closing turn:** Myeongha requests a private meeting with Seorin before the Compact hearing.
